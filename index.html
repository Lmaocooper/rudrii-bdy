Dear Rudri,

There are people who arrive in your life loudly, and then there are people who somehow become part of the furniture of your heart.

This is a very long way of saying: I am really, really glad you exist.

So I made you a little place on the internet. Not because the internet needs another website, obviously, but because you deserve something that feels handmade.

Something you can wander through.

Something that keeps little pieces of you safe.

And because I know you, there are going to be things here that make you laugh, things that make you roll your eyes, and probably at least one thing that makes you say, “what is wrong with you?”

— me, obviously
